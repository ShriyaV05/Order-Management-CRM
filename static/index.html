Safe Recovery Workflow & Security Notice

All backups contain full, consistent snapshots of all historical orders, customer records, inventory, settings, and audit logs. Per POPCONE CRM Stage 1 safety protocols, direct in-place production database overwrite is strictly disabled to prevent accidental data corruption or loss.

To recover or inspect historical data: Decrypt the archive using your secure offline key, replay the snapshot into an isolated sandbox database, and verify complete schema and relational integrity.
